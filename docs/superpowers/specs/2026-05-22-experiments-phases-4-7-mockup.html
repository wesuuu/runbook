Recorded outcome

Conclusion

The 7 g/L glucose setpoint produced the highest day-12 titer at 2.61 g/L (+24% vs. baseline), exceeding the 15% success criterion. At 8 g/L the titer regressed to 2.42 g/L, consistent with substrate-inhibition.

Recommendation: adopt 7 g/L as the new default setpoint pending one confirmation run on a fresh seed train.
