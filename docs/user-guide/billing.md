# Billing and plans

Batchrite subscriptions are managed per organization. Only organization admins can view and change billing details. All billing settings are found in Settings under the **Billing** tab.

## What you can do

- View your organization's current plan and subscription status.
- See how many seats are in use relative to your plan's limit.
- Compare available subscription tiers.
- Upgrade, downgrade, or reactivate your subscription.
- Add or update a payment method.
- View invoice history and cancel through the billing portal.

## The subscription tiers

Batchrite offers three plans:

**Essentials** — The included tier for new organizations. Provides core protocol editing, experiment execution, and run management.

**Pro** — Advanced features for teams. Includes everything in Essentials plus higher seat limits and Pro-only capabilities such as AI model configuration and GLP mode.

**Enterprise** — Custom deployment, SLA, and dedicated support. Contact [sales@batchrite.com](mailto:sales@batchrite.com) to get started.

No prices are displayed in the application. Contact the sales team or open the billing portal to see current pricing.

## How to manage your subscription

1. Open **Settings** from the main navigation and click the **Billing** tab.
2. The top card shows your current plan name and status (for example, Active, Trialing, or Past due). It also shows how many seats your organization is using.
3. To change your plan, scroll to the **Plans** section. Click **Upgrade** next to Pro to move up, or **Downgrade** to return to Essentials. Both buttons open the billing portal.
4. To update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel, click **Open billing portal** in the **Manage billing** card.
5. If your subscription is in a trial, the page shows how many days remain and a button to **Add payment method** before the trial ends.
6. If your subscription has lapsed, click **Re-subscribe** to reactivate it through the billing portal. While lapsed, reads and exports remain available but new changes are blocked.
7. If your seat count exceeds your plan's limit, a warning appears with an **Upgrade** button and a link to **Manage members**.
