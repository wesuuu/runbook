# Getting started with Batchrite

Batchrite is a Laboratory Execution System (LES) built for biotech process development scientists. It is a tablet-friendly digital lab notebook where you organise your work into projects, define reusable protocols, and execute experiments as runs — all with a complete audit trail. Everything is stored in a graph-based structure so you can track deviations, compare versions, and keep your team aligned from any device.

## What you can do

- **Dashboard** — see your active and planned runs at a glance, and track your team's recent activity.
- **Projects** — group related protocols, experiments, and runs into a single workspace. Each project has tabs for Protocols, Experiments, All Runs, Activity, and Settings.
- **Library** — upload and search reference documents (SOPs, datasheets, papers). The AI can search the Library to answer science questions.
- **AI Chat** — open a full-page conversation with the Batchrite AI assistant to design protocols, explore the Library, or ask product questions.
- **Settings** — manage your organization, teams, Sites & Equipment, profile, appearance, notifications, AI Models, templates, and billing.

## How to find your way around

The navigation bar runs across the top of every page. On desktop you will see:

1. Click **Dashboard** to return to the home overview.
2. Click **Library** to browse and upload reference documents.
3. Click **AI Chat** to open the full-page AI assistant.
4. Click **Projects** (or the current project name) to open the Projects dropdown, then choose a project or click **View All Projects** to see the full list.
5. Click your avatar in the top-right corner to open the user menu, which gives you access to **Settings** and **Sign Out**.

On a phone or tablet, tap the hamburger icon (three horizontal lines) on the left of the nav bar to open the mobile menu. It shows the same links: Dashboard, Library, AI Chat, Projects, and Settings.

## How to get help

A floating chat button appears at the bottom-right of most pages. Click it to open the **Trellis AI** panel without leaving your current page. You can:

1. Type a question in the text box and press **Enter** (or click the send button) to ask.
2. Use the keyboard shortcut **Cmd+J** (Mac) or **Ctrl+J** (Windows/Linux) to toggle the panel open or closed.
3. Click the expand icon in the panel header to switch to the full **AI Chat** page for a larger workspace.
4. Click the refresh icon in the panel header to clear the conversation and start fresh.

The AI assistant can answer questions about how Batchrite works, help you design protocols, and search your Library documents. If the floating button is not visible on a page (for example, inside the protocol editor), navigate to **AI Chat** from the top nav bar instead.
