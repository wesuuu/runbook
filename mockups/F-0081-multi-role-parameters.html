No tab strip. The role context bar + role-grouped aside together do everything a tab strip would have done, with less chrome.

  • Where am I? Role context bar shows the active role's name, ordinal, and stats with the role color as a vertical bar.
  • How do I move linearly? The ‹ 1 / 3 › nav in the context bar — one-tap on tablet.
  • How do I jump? The aside's role group headers are clickable. Click "Senior Operator" in the aside → cards filter to that role, context bar updates, the clicked group highlights.
  • How do I see edits across roles? The aside is always global — never changes when you switch roles. That's the constant orientation point.
  • State preservation — overrides on inactive roles stay in currentGraph; switching is a presentational filter, not a remount. The aside's per-role count proves this.
  • Role color — small dot driven by ProtocolRole.color. Used as a mark, not a fill. Mint stays the sole override signal.
